Your promot should have
1. Task
2. Context
3. Clear output

Advance prompt tips
1. Give examples
2. Provide persona:  you could tell a Gen AI tool to act as a Chief Marketing Officer, a McKinsey Senior Partner, a 32-year old mom from Chicago, or Steve Jobs.
3. Describe tone:  make an email "more professional," or to try out different communication styles by asking it to make different versions of a speech you wrote in a motivational, reassuring, or serious voice.
